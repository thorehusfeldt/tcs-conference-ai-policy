Submissions

The submissions are done via Easychair, by following the link
https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=stacs2027 

Format:

- Authors are invited to submit an extended abstract or a full paper with at
  most 15 pages; this page limit excludes the title page, the references
  section, and a possible appendix. The title page should contain the title of
  the paper and the abstract, but no author information. The first section of
  the paper should start on the next page, and the appendix, if any, should
  also start on the next page after the bibliography.In preparing submissions,
  the usage of the LIPIcs style file is mandatory; no changes to font size,
  page geometry, etc. are permitted. Please refer to LIPIcs author
  instructions. Submissions submitted after the deadline will not be
  considered, and submissions not adhering to the expected format risk
  rejection without consideration of the content.
- Submissions should be made to appropriate tracks. The PCs reserve the right
  to reassign a paper to a different track, if deemed necessary.
- The extended abstract should contain a succinct statement of the considered
  issues and of their motivation, a summary of the main results, and a brief
  explanation of their significance, accessible to non-specialist readers.
  This should be followed by a rigorous derivation of the claimed results.
  Proofs omitted due to space constraints should be put into an appendix, to
  be read by the program committee members at their discretion. It is allowed
  and encouraged to provide the anonymized full version of the paper as the
  appendix.
- Simultaneous submission to other conferences with published proceedings or
  to journals is not allowed. PC members are allowed to submit their works as
  well, except for PC chairs.                                                 
           

Conflicts of interest: The authors are required to declare a conflict of
interest with PC-members in the following situations (in case of any doubts
whether a COI should be declared or not, please do not hesitate to contact the
PC-co-chairs):

- the PC-member is a family member or close friend of the author.
- the PC-member is the Ph.D. advisor or an advisee (no time limit) of the
  author, or postdoctoral or undergraduate mentor or mentee within the past
  five years.
- the PC-member is a person with the same affiliation as the author.
- the PC-member and the author were involved in an alleged incident of
  harassment. (It is not required that the incident was reported.)
- the PC-member owes the author a favor (e.g., recently requested a reference
  letter) or vice-versa.
- the PC-member is a frequent or recent collaborator (within last 3 years) of
  the author, and thus cannot be expected to objectively review the author's
  work.

Double-blind reviewing:

- STACS 2027 will employ a lightweight double-blind reviewing process:
  submissions should not reveal the identity of the authors in any way. The
  names of the authors will be revealed to the PC-members of the track where
  the paper is submitted, but not to any external sub-reviewers, in order to
  make the detection of potential conflicts of interest more effective.
- The purpose of the double-blind reviewing is to help reviewers come to an
  initial judgment about the paper without bias, not to make it impossible for
  them to discover the authors if they were to try. Nothing should be done in
  the name of anonymity that weakens the submission or makes the job of
  reviewing the paper more difficult. In particular, important references
  should not be omitted or anonymized.
- In addition, authors are encouraged to disseminate their ideas or draft
  versions of their paper as they normally would (e.g., give talks on their
  research ideas). In particular, authors are strongly encouraged to post
  their submitted papers on the web, upload them to arXiv or any public
  repository.

Rebuttal:                                             

- There will be a rebuttal period for authors. Authors will receive the
  reviews of their submissions and have a few days to prepare and submit
  rebuttals. These rebuttals become part of the PC discussions, but entail no
  specific responses. Rebuttals will be handled through EasyChair.           
                            

Information on the use of generative AI (including LLMs):

- STACS submissions will have to follow the rules set out in the ACM policy on
  authorship
- Moreover, the use of AI tools for conducting research must be explicitly
  disclosed and discussed in all submitted papers, with their specific uses
  described in detail. The authors of a submission will be held responsible
  and accountable for any problematic content contained in the respective
  submission, regardless of its source.
- Reviewers are asked to adhere to the LIPIcs rules regarding the usage of
  generative AI (https://drops.dagstuhl.de/docs/gen-ai) and are fully
  responsible for the contents of their reviews. PC members and subreferees
  must preserve confidentiality of the submitted material; in particular, the
  usage of external AI tools should be avoided.
